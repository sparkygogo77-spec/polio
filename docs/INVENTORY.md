# The unfinished inventory

Everything this repository started and cannot yet finish, as one ranked
list. Ranked by what a person would notice first, not by what is easiest;
within one level of noticeability the cheapest to close comes first, because
that is the order they are being closed in.

Swept at `7b38e86` (`claude/template-ui-website-builder-11bfgl`), 15 September
2026, on a fresh `npm run build` of that commit, by one session whose only job
was this list. Every row was confirmed by reading the code it names, and the
rows marked *live* were also driven in a browser against the build. Rows in
another session's area are listed and flagged, not edited.

Six kinds were swept, and the number found of each:

| kind | found |
|---|---|
| 1. controls that do nothing, or not what the label says | 24 (A1 to A3, A6 to A12, A14 to A20, A22 to A24; more inside A16 and A20) |
| 2. declarations left behind and unused | 8 groups, 160 items (E1 to E8) |
| 3. routes and paths answering with a stub or a constant | 19 (B1 to B19) |
| 4. reachable in one mode and not another, or offered and not takeable | 17 (A4, A5, A12, A13, A21, B20 to B31) |
| 5. checks that pass while measuring something else | 63 checks in 27 suites (D1 to D38), plus 5 suites with 0 checks |
| 6. one shape of input works, another quietly degrades | 26 rows, 41 shapes (C1 to C26) |

Columns: **file** · **what it promises** · **what it actually does** · **cost
to close** (S under an hour, M half a day, L a day or more) · **status**.
Rows a person notices first are at the top.
